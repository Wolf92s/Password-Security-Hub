Mastering Your Digital Security

Welcome to your guide for creating an unbreakable foundation for your online life. The single most important step you can take is using a strong, unique password for every single website and service. This guide will show you how.

Why Every Password MUST Be Different

Hackers rely on a technique called "credential stuffing." When a website like 'SocialMediaSite A' gets breached, attackers take the list of emails and passwords and try them on other sites—your bank, your email, shopping sites, etc. If you reuse the same password, a breach on one unimportant site can lead to a complete takeover of your digital life.

The solution is to use a password manager to generate and store dozens of complex, unique passwords. But that manager needs to be protected by one, ultra-strong remembered password. That's where this site comes in.

Creating Your Unbreakable Master Password

Your master password should be two things: memorable to you and impossible for anyone else to guess. For best security pratices you should use a stronger password than say "Password1". If you have trouble remembering try using passphrases, examples below.

  • A good passphrase is a sequence of words, often with spaces or special characters.
  • It's long, which makes it incredibly hard to crack.
  • It's easier to remember than a random string like `8!k&z@p#`.

// Examples of strong, memorable passphrases:
MyDogLoves$leepingInTheSun!
Correct-Horse-Battery-Staple-99
4sc0re&7years-ago

Come up with your own unique passphrase. Then, head over to our Generator. Type your passphrase into the "Remembered Password" box and select your encryption options. The output will be the secure, complex string you should use as the remembered password for your password manager.
